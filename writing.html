How Singapore finances public investment

Tax revenue, public wealth and lessons for the United Kingdom.

Why can Singapore fund extensive infrastructure while keeping personal income tax relatively low? This project looks beyond headline tax rates to public investment returns, state-owned assets and compulsory household saving, then compares Singapore’s approach with the UK’s revenue base and spending commitments. The next stage will test how these financing arrangements translate into infrastructure outcomes and productivity.
