01 · Investment thesis

Why the base case points to upside

Cash generation creates flexibilityStrong operating cash flow and net cash support dividends, reinvestment and valuation resilience.
Cigarettes remain the earnings engineHigh segment profitability continues to anchor group earnings and cash generation.
FMCG provides structural growthHigher growth outside cigarettes offers a credible route to greater earnings diversification.
Diversification adds optionalityAgri, paper and other businesses provide multiple revenue and earnings drivers.

08 · Risks

What could break the thesis

Tobacco taxation & regulation

Policy changes could weaken the group's largest profit pool.

FMCG competition

Competitive intensity can delay margin expansion.

Commodity input costs

Volatility can pressure consumer and paper economics.

Paper cyclicality

Pricing and demand can move sharply through cycles.

Valuation sensitivity

Small changes to WACC, terminal growth and margins materially move fair value.

Historical comparability

The Hotels demerger and corporate restructurings complicate trend analysis.

09 · Final view

BUY · ₹372 blended target

Against a ₹263.50 reference price on 9 September 2026, the blended valuation implies approximately 41% upside under the base-case assumptions.
