OCS / Mitie — £3.1bn M&A Transaction Analysis

An analysis of OCS's recommended acquisition of Mitie, focusing on the premium, strategic rationale, financing and merger economics.

View project
Detailed synopsis

The project asks whether the strategic value of combining OCS and Mitie justifies the headline takeover premium and whether the enlarged group can support the financing required to execute the transaction.

It examines transaction structure, Mitie's standalone quality, strategic fit, the sponsor-backed financing package, illustrative synergy cases, leverage and deleveraging, before reaching an independent judgement on whether the price is defensible.

ITC Limited — Equity Research & Valuation

An independent valuation of ITC using DCF, sum-of-the-parts, relative valuation and exploratory econometric analysis.

View project
Detailed synopsis

The research evaluates ITC as a diversified Indian consumer business, combining fundamental company analysis with a five-year FCFF DCF, segment-based SOTP valuation and a forward P/E cross-check.

An accompanying regression explores ITC monthly returns alongside RBI repo-rate changes and NIFTY returns, with the econometric results treated as exploratory rather than causal.

Financial regulation and competitive balance in English football

A World Studies Extended Essay combining mathematics and economics to test whether football financial regulation improves competitive balance.

Read paper
Detailed synopsis

This IB World Studies Extended Essay is an interdisciplinary research paper combining mathematics and economics to investigate UEFA Financial Fair Play and the Premier League's Profit and Sustainability Rules.

It applies economic ideas around market structure and barriers to entry alongside quantitative measures including the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index, concentration ratios and competitive-balance measures to assess whether regulation produced a sustained improvement in competition.
